What we do

A coffee house and a bakery, in one room

Breakfast starts at eight — omelettes and scrambles made with fresh organic eggs, manakeesh out of the oven, croissants filled to order. The espresso bar runs all day: Turkish, cortado, Spanish latte, and a frappé list for the summer.

By lunch the kitchen is on: Neapolitan and Americano pizza, pasta by the plate, burgers, salads built properly. It keeps going until midnight, which in Maadi matters.

And there is always something baked. Cheesecake, molten chocolate cake, brownies on ice, and the croissants that started it.
